# Brief: Band 8.5+ Strategy Guide

**Goal:** One compact, easy guide inside IELTS Studio that tells a strong learner exactly how to approach every section, task and question type to reach Band 8.5+. Just enough to act on, with nothing to wade through.

**Research first:**
- Official sources: IELTS.org / British Council / IDP pages on test format, the public band descriptors (Writing and Speaking Band 8–9 wording), and the official "test-taking tips".
- Well-known teaching approaches (e.g. IELTS Liz, IELTS Simon, E2, British Council *Road to IELTS*): how they teach each question type.
- Summarise in our own words. Never copy text. Link to the sources.

**For every skill and question type, give exactly:**
1. **Look at it:** what to notice first (instructions, word limit, order of answers).
2. **Steps:** 3–5 numbered actions.
3. **Do / Don't:** two short lists.
4. **Memory trick:** one mnemonic or rule of thumb.
5. **Worked example:** an original mini question, the thinking, and the answer.

**Also include:**
- "What Band 8.5 looks like" per skill, paraphrasing the descriptors (e.g. Reading/Listening ≈ 37–39/40; Writing "fully extended", "rare errors"; Speaking "fluent with only rare repetition").
- An exam-day checklist (before, during and after each paper).
- Time plans per paper.

**In the app:**
- A **Strategy Guide** page with tabs per skill and an expandable card per question type.
- A "How to approach this" link next to each question group in Reading and Listening, opening the matching card.
- A "Tip of the day" on the Dashboard, drawn from the memory tricks.

**Constraints:** Short sentences. No walls of text. Original examples only. Mark official facts versus teaching advice. Keep content as data (`src/content/strategy.ts`) so it's testable and easy to edit.
